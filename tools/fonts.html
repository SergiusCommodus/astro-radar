Font trial

Six display faces against our current Barlow Condensed, on the page background, at the sizes the hero uses. This page is a trial only: no font is applied to the site. File sizes are each font's latin subset (WOFF2) as Google Fonts serves it, measured on November 9, 2026. The A column says whether the capital A has no crossbar (the look of the Astro mark).

FontWeightLatin WOFF2Capital ALicence

Result: none of the six has a capital A without a crossbar (checked on the 96 px A of each, below; Michroma's bar sits low and Bruno Ace's A is an arch with a bar). A crossbarless A would have to stay a drawing in the Astro mark, not come from a font. Smallest: Orbitron (6.4 KB) and Oxanium (8.7 KB), both smaller than Barlow Condensed 600 (21.8 KB).

Licences are as listed on Google Fonts (each family's page and its OFL.txt). Check the OFL.txt shipped with the files before self hosting any of them. The site's own fonts stay in assets/fonts/ under the same licence.
