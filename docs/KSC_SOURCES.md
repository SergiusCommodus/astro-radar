# Sources for astroradar.io/ksc

The Florida page (`ksc_page` in `launch_pages.py`) shows only what is below. Everything else about these places (hours, fees, parking, what is allowed) is left out on purpose and the page sends people to the official page instead. Checked November 9, 2026.

## Verified

| Fact on the page | Source |
|---|---|
| Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex offers three launch viewing areas: the Main Visitor Complex (North Atlantis Lawn, next to Space Shuttle Atlantis), the Banana Creek Launch Viewing Area at the Apollo/Saturn V Center, and the Gantry at LC-39 | [kennedyspacecenter.com, Launch Viewing Locations](https://kennedyspacecenter.com/launches-and-events/see-a-launch/launch-viewing-locations) |
| Their distances to the pads: Main Visitor Complex about 7.1 mi (11.4 km) to SLC-41, 6.7 mi (10.8 km) to SLC-40, 7.5 mi (12 km) to LC-39A; Banana Creek about 5.4 mi (8.7 km) to SLC-41, 6.2 mi (10 km) to SLC-40, 3.9 mi (6.27 km) to LC-39A; the Gantry about 2.3 mi (3.7 km) to SLC-41 and 3.4 mi (5.5 km) to SLC-40, with no viewing there for LC-39A and LC-39B launches | same page |
| The Gantry is sold in addition to daily admission | same page |
| Viewing availability depends on the launch, and the Visitor Complex lists only officially announced launch dates on its event pages | same page; [How to watch a rocket launch](https://www.kennedyspacecenter.com/blog/03/how-to-watch-a-rocket-launch) |
| Playalinda Beach (Canaveral National Seashore) can have temporary closures at the request of NASA Launch Security | [NPS news release, March 18, 2026](https://www.nps.gov/cana/learn/news/cananeral-national-seashore-artemis-ii-press-release.htm) |
| For an LC-39A launch the Park Service closed all its areas from parking area 13 south to the Playalinda Road Gate, reopening after a successful launch or a scrub | [NPS news release, June 2, 2021](https://www.nps.gov/cana/learn/news/launch-closure.htm) |
| Locations of Space View Park (Titusville), Jetty Park (Cape Canaveral), Playalinda Beach and the Visitor Complex | OpenStreetMap through Nominatim: way 143515412, way 296954741, way 215666051, way 166133965 (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) |
| Distances from those places to the next launch's pad | worked out by the build from the OpenStreetMap locations and the pad coordinates in the launch feed, through the page's one distance rule |

## Left out (could not be verified from an official or reliable source)

- Cocoa Beach Pier, the SR 528 causeway, Max Brewer Bridge, Parrish Park, Sand Point Park, the Cape Canaveral Lighthouse area: named by news sites and guides, not by an official page that could be read.
- Any hours, fees, parking rules or arrival advice for any place.
- The Space Coast Office of Tourism's launch viewing map: its page names no places in its text (the map is an external Google map).
