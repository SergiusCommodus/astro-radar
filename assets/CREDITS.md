# Image credits

## Globe hero (`globe-day.webp`, `globe-night.webp`)

NASA imagery, not protected by copyright in the United States. NASA's Earth Observatory and Visible Earth ask for credit; the only
mandatory credit is NASA (https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/ImageUse, https://www.visibleearth.nasa.gov/image-use-policy).
The page credits "Globe imagery: NASA" in the footer.

- `globe-day.webp`: Blue Marble Next Generation, December 2004, topography and bathymetry
  (https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73909/world.topo.bathy.200412.3x5400x2700.jpg).
  Credit: NASA Earth Observatory, Reto Stöckli, based on data from NASA and NOAA.
- `globe-night.webp`: Black Marble 2016, 3 km
  (https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_3km.jpg).
  Credit: NASA Earth Observatory images by Joshua Stevens, using Suomi NPP VIIRS data from Miguel Román, NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center.

Both were resized to 2048 by 1024 (equirectangular, Lanczos) and saved as WebP (day quality 76, 171 KB; night quality 80, 66 KB) with
Pillow. To redo them, download the two files above and run the same resize.
