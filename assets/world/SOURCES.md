# Live world textures: sources

Every texture here is made from NASA imagery or data NASA publishes. NASA imagery is not protected by copyright in the United States; NASA asks for credit (https://science.nasa.gov/image-use-policy/, https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software-policies). GEBCO's grid, which NASA republishes in the Blue Marble collection, is in the public domain and free to use with credit (https://www.gebco.net/data_and_products/gridded_bathymetry_data/). No paid or unlicensed imagery or model is used. The page credits NASA in the footer.

`scripts/build_world_textures.py <folder>` makes every file here from the downloads below (put them, named as they are, in one folder). Downloaded 2026-11-03.

How each was made (all resized with Lanczos and saved as WebP):

- Day: resized as it is; no relief or color change (the page's shader lights the relief from the height map). (The 8K tiles for the old close range limb view are gone: the globe card sees the planet from far enough out that the 4K map is sharp.)
- Night: Black Marble's luminance minus the dim land and sea floor around it (a local minimum, blurred), stretched, grey. The page colors it.
- Relief: red is height (the sea floor from 0 to 127 by depth, land from 128 to 255 on a square root scale of elevation), green is water (255) or land (0).
- Clouds: for each satellite, how much whiter than the Blue Marble of the same month a pixel is and how grey it is; the median of the satellites that saw it (sun glint and swath gaps fall out), gaps no satellite covered filled from around them, and an even soft cover toward the poles, where the November mosaics are dark or icy. The page adds moving noise detail so the clouds change shape as they drift.

Not used: the old Blue Marble cloud map (cloud_combined) is no longer on NASA's servers, and the old eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov host did not answer from here.
